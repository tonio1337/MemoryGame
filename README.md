It's a memory card game built with React.

You have 16 cards face down on a grid. You click one card to flip it and see an emoji, then click another. If both emojis match, they stay flipped and you get a point. If they don't match, they flip back over after 1 second. You keep going until all 8 pairs are found — then a "Play Again" button appears to restart.

It tracks your score (how many pairs found) and your moves (how many times you've picked 2 cards). The cards are shuffled randomly every new game so it's different each time.
